Who We Are

The Neighborhood Complaint Management System is a digital platform designed to help residents report community issues quickly and efficiently. Our goal is to create a cleaner, safer, and more connected neighborhood by providing a simple way for citizens to communicate local concerns to authorities.

Whether it's a road problem, water supply issue, sanitation concern, security matter, or any other community-related problem, our system ensures that complaints are recorded, tracked, and monitored until resolution.

Easy Reporting

Submit complaints quickly through a user-friendly interface.

Complaint Tracking

Track complaint progress using a unique complaint ID.

Faster Resolution

Improve communication and speed up issue resolution.
