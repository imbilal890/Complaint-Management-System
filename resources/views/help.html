🛟 Help & Support

How to Submit a Complaint

Go to the Report Issue page, select a complaint category, describe your issue, and submit the form.

How to Track a Complaint

Open the Track Complaint page and enter your Complaint ID (e.g., CMP001) to view status.

Complaint Status
  • Pending: Complaint received
  • In Progress: Work is ongoing
  • Resolved: Issue fixed
Need Help?

Contact your local authority or system administrator for support.
