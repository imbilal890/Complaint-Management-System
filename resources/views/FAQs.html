❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Smart Neighborhood CMS?
It is a system that allows citizens to report local issues and track their resolution online.
How can I submit a complaint?
Go to the Report Issue page, fill the form, and submit your complaint.
How do I track my complaint?
Enter your Complaint ID on the Track page to see status updates.
Who manages complaints?
The admin panel manages all complaints and updates their status.
